Web hosting and deployment

When you build a website, you create files like HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and images.

But for other people to see your site, those files must be stored somewhere online and served to their browsers when they visit your site’s address.

That’s the job of a web hosting service — it stores your files and makes them accessible to the internet 24/7.

There are many types of hosting, but for this course, we focus on static hosting. This means we are hosting only the front-end, files like .html, .css, and .js, not back-end code or databases
